# A Completionist’s Checklist: Ocarina of Time (Remake)

A spoiler-light, 100% completion checklist for *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* on Nintendo Switch 2.
It walks through everything in an order that avoids as much backtracking as possible, while leaving the
exploring to you.

**Live site:** https://magik-bean.github.io/OoT-checklist/

## What it does
- **29 chapters in play order**: every Gold Skulltula, Heart Piece, Heart Container, song, bottle,
  Great Fairy, upgrade and side quest needed for 100%.
- **Spoiler-light lines**: each line says what and roughly where. Tap it to reveal the details, or tap the
  camera for a screenshot.
- **Quest status**: hearts that fill by quarters, a Gold Skulltula meter with every token reward, and a
  Triforce that fills with your overall progress.
- **Items & Equipment**: N64-style Select Item and Equipment screens that fill in as you go. Swipe sideways to turn between them.
- **Navi**: lost, or picking up after a break? Tap her (“Where was I?”) to jump to your next unchecked line.
- **Notes**: a notepad for things like your Scarecrow tune, plus notes inside each chapter.
- **Filters and search**: Skulltulas, Heart Pieces, Songs, Bottles and Great Fairies, Items, Incomplete
  chapters, Hide done, and a search bar that stays with you as you scroll.
- **Works offline** once it has loaded, in light or dark mode.

## Install it like an app
- **iPhone / iPad:** open the link in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap **⋮**, then **Install app**.
- **Windows / Mac:** Chrome and Edge show an install icon in the address bar.

## Your progress
Progress saves automatically on each device. To move it to another device, scroll to the bottom of the
page, choose **Back up or restore**, copy the code, and paste it on the other device.
